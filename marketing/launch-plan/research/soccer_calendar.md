# JaxSquares soccer calendar research, Oct 24 to Nov 29, 2026

Compiled Friday, Oct 23, 2026, for the Instagram calendar. On a soccer board every square is a minute of the match and each goal's minute wins, so any match with a big US audience works. Thanksgiving 2026 is **Thursday, Nov 26**.

## Research status (read first)

- **Verified:** 54 of the 56 table rows have a verified date: an official league, club, confederation or broadcaster page lists it, or two reputable outlets agree. In 31 of those 54, the time, venue or US channel is still TBD or unconfirmed, and the Status column says which. 2 rows are Unconfirmed outright.
- **How it was checked:** about 32 web searches. WebFetch could not open any page (DNS errors on mlssoccer.com and premierleague.com), and a direct fetch got a proxy 403. Every "Verified" row rests on search-result summaries of the URLs listed, not on reading the page itself. **Before a post goes out, open the source and check the time.**
- **What's missing:** most US TV assignments. NBC names its Premier League channels in a press release the week of the match, and CBS/Paramount+ names the Champions League match on CBS Sports Network/Golazo about a week out. Rows marked "TBD" are not announced yet, not guesses. Also missing: the exact game-by-game dates for MLS Round One, NWSL quarterfinal/semifinal matchups and times, Liga MX kickoff times and US channels, and LaLiga's US channel.
- **Clock changes inside the window:** the UK and Europe go back an hour on **Sun Oct 25**, the US on **Sun Nov 1**. UK-to-ET gap: 5 hours through Oct 24, **4 hours Oct 25 to Oct 31**, 5 hours from Nov 1. Central Europe (Spain, UEFA's CET) to ET: 5 hours Oct 25 to Oct 31, 6 hours from Nov 1. Every ET time below was converted with those gaps.
- **Fixtures move for TV and cup runs.** Premier League and Champions League dates here are the current official ones, but both leagues warn of short-notice changes.

1. **Sun Oct 25, El Clásico, 4:00 p.m. ET.** The biggest club match in the world on a Sunday afternoon, and the calendar's first soccer weekend. Launch the "every square is a minute" idea here.
2. **Sat Oct 24, Chelsea v Tottenham, 12:30 p.m. ET (Peacock, NBCSN, Telemundo).** A London derby and NBC's headline game, the day before the Clásico. A "two-board weekend" story.
3. **Sat Oct 31, Chelsea v Man United, 8:30 a.m. ET.** A Halloween-morning coffee board with two of the most-followed clubs in the US.
4. **Tue Nov 3 and Wed Nov 4, Champions League Matchday 4, 12:45 and 3:00 p.m. ET.** Barcelona v Aston Villa, Man United v Roma, Atlético v Bayern, Fenerbahçe v Liverpool, Leipzig v Man City. Weekday afternoons are perfect for office boards.
5. **Sat Nov 7, MLS Decision Day, 4:00 and 7:00 p.m. ET.** All 30 clubs at once, with playoff spots decided by late goals. Ties in with the "late-minute squares" angle.
6. **Sat Nov 21, NWSL Championship, 8:00 p.m. ET on CBS.** A final in prime time on network TV from Washington, D.C.: the biggest US-league soccer final in the window.
7. **Sun Nov 22, Liverpool v Man United, 11:30 a.m. ET.** England's fiercest rivalry, on a Sunday late morning before NFL kickoffs.
8. **Tue Nov 17, USMNT v Haiti in Cincinnati, 7:00 p.m. ET.** The national team on home soil, a Nations League knockout, the only US soccer on a quiet international-break week. (Nov 14 first leg: confirm venue and time first.)
9. **Tue Nov 24 and Wed Nov 25, Champions League Matchday 5 (Thanksgiving week).** Arsenal v Dortmund, Man City v Napoli, PSG v Roma, Sporting v Man United, Lille v Bayern, all at 3:00 p.m. ET. A pre-holiday office board.
10. **Thu Nov 26 to Fri Nov 27, Thanksgiving soccer.** Liga MX quarterfinal first legs (Nov 25-26), USWNT v El Salvador in the W Championship (Fri Nov 27, a World Cup qualifier), Nottingham Forest v Chelsea on Black Friday at 3:00 p.m. ET.
11. **Sun Nov 29, the Premier League "super Sunday."** The Merseyside derby at 7:00 a.m. ET (an early riser's board) and Arsenal v Man City at 11:30 a.m. ET (champions v leaders). The same weekend has the Liga MX quarterfinal second legs and MLS Round One playoff games.
12. **Wed Nov 18 to Fri Nov 20, MLS Wild Card and Round One start.** A one-game knockout midweek, then the best-of-3 series begin. Tease MLS Cup on Fri Dec 18.
