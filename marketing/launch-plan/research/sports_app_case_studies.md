## 1. Comparable sports, fantasy, pick'em and social-game apps: what drove growth

### Sleeper (fantasy leagues built around friend group chat)
- **[V]** Positioning: from the start Sleeper presented itself as a *social platform* for a league rather than a stats tool, and as "simple and free, ad-free". Sources: TechCrunch, 2017, https://techcrunch.com/?p=1868752; Expa founder spotlight, Feb 21, 2023, https://expa.com/news/founder-spotlight-sleeper.
- **[V]** Chat is on nearly every screen. The CEO calls the app "a place to hang out with your friends". Its avatars can wear team jerseys. Sources: a16z Series B post, https://a16z.com/?p=17335; Expa (above).
- **[V]** Growth numbers: a16z reported leagues up **700% year over year** and more than **1M active players** in that season. The post is undated in the extract (it covers the Series B era). A 2025-era job listing says the app serves "over 10 million users", which is a company claim. Source: https://a16z.com/?p=17335.
- **[U]** "Millions of users with zero ad spend" appears in a third-party profile only: https://yespress.io/sleeper.
- **[V]** The current social operation can be read off job posts:
  - A "Performance Creative Associate (TikTok Ads)" makes trend- and meme-driven short videos ("edgy campaigns that drive user engagement and app downloads"). Posted Apr 2025: https://jobs.generalcatalyst.com/companies/sleeper/jobs/48435954-performance-creative-associate-tiktok-ads
  - Team-specific TikTok curators post **5–7 videos a week per NFL team** and keep up community engagement: https://startup.jobs/sleeper-tiktok-nfl-team-curator-sleeper-7405491
  - A growth intern recruits creators into an affiliate program: https://builtincharlotte.com/job/growth-marketing-intern/6232757
- **Lesson [I]:** Sleeper's growth engine was the product being social, with marketing amplifying it. It now runs a volume of trend-native short video and splits accounts by community (one TikTok per team). For JaxSquares, the parallel would be content series built around specific games (for example "Bears–Lions board") rather than one generic feed.

### Underdog (DFS / pick'em)
- **[V]** Underdog's Shorty Awards entry is self-reported. It says the 2025 goals were to become "a leading creator-powered digital sports brand", to act as a real-time DFS news source, and to build short-form video across platforms. It reports a network of about **4.8M followers**, including The Arena on YouTube at about 1.23M and @underdogfantasy on TikTok at about 336.5K. The Arena won an audience honor in the Sports Podcast category. Source: https://shortyawards.com/18th/underdog-3
- **[V]** The social associate role covers daily posting on X, TikTok and Instagram, weekends included, plus live game coverage: https://dynamitejobs.com/company/underdogfantasy/remote-job/social-media-associate
- **Lesson [I]:** Their reach comes from talent (analysts and former players) and being always on during games. JaxSquares can borrow the live, in-game posting rhythm through Stories during the big games, but cannot afford the talent model. Micro-creators can stand in for it (see Section 4).

### PrizePicks
- **[V]** The creator strategy favors "smart and efficient" partnerships over chasing the "biggest names possible", which the company says brings high cost for little return. Named partners are comedian Druski and creator Trent Attyah. Source: Gambling Insider interview, https://www.gamblinginsider.com/?p=91332
- **[V]** The social team is split between **Editorial** (always on) and **"Gametime"**, which covers live, reactive moments in sports and culture. Source: https://www.teamworkonline.com/sports-technology-jobs/prize-picks/prizepicks-jobs/social-media-coordinator-gametime-2189798
- **[V]** "The Feed" (around Nov 20, 2025) lets users copy ("tail") the lineups of friends and creators, and produce shareable links for social or DMs. PrizePicks research found younger users would rather follow or fade friends than shop pre-made lineups. Sources: https://www.ingame.com/?p=34553 and https://www.saturdaydownsouth.com/news/dfs/prizepicks-debuts-new-social-feature-the-feed/
- **Lesson [I]:** Social comparison inside a friend group ("tail or fade my buddy") is the hook. For squares the equivalent is "look at my square vs yours" and "who's sweating Q4".

### ESPN Fantasy and the ESPN Tournament Challenge
- **[V]** The "Only in Fantasy" campaign, made with agency BSSP, leaned on **smack talk** between league-mates. The 2023 run went from Jul 17 to Sep 10. Source: https://www.gamblinginsider.com/news/21814/espn-launches-new-fantasy-football-marketing-campaign
- **[V]** Bracket entries have hit records repeatedly:
  - 24.4M men's brackets in 2025, a third straight record. Source: https://espnpressroom.com/us/press-releases/2025/03/24-4-million-brackets-espn-tournament-challenge-sets-new-record-for-third-consecutive-year/
  - 18.8M in 2017, which ESPN tied to a 24-hour "Tournament Challenge Marathon" content push: https://espnpressroom.com/us/press-releases/2017/03/espn-tournament-challenge-explodes-record-18-8-million-brackets/
  - The women's challenge reached 3.25M in 2024, up 56%: https://awfulannouncing.com/espn/mens-tournament-challenge-record-24-million-brackets.html
- **Lesson [I]:** Seasonal deadline content ("lock it before tip-off") combined with a concentrated content blitz moves entries. Squares has the same shape: "board locks at kickoff".

### Yahoo Fantasy
- **[V]** For 2025 Yahoo launched guillotine leagues co-branded with Liquid Death, built on dark humor, custom avatars and animations. Liquid Death framed it as "connecting with your coworkers, friends and family". Source: https://news.designrush.com/yahoo-sports-liquid-death-fantasy-guillotine-league
- **[V]** On Aug 3, 2026 Yahoo launched a college fantasy campaign fronted by Robert Griffin III. I saw the title only. Source: https://unified.www.yahooinc.com/press/robert-griffin-iii-goes-back-to-school-kicking-off-new-yahoo-college-fantasy-football-campaign

### theScore and team accounts (meme-led sports social)
- **[V]** theScore passed 1.4M Instagram followers, gaining about 400K in a year, and credits original content plus live event coverage. One retro-jersey post reached about 2M users. All company-reported: https://thescore.com/news/1862809. In 2020 it reported roughly 100M monthly social reach and fast TikTok growth: https://mediaincanada.com/?p=144046
- **[V]** The LA Chargers' Instagram win-memes drew about **3.6M impressions and about 7K new followers** (Shorty entry): https://shortyawards.com/14th/charger-social-media. The NJ Devils became "meme lords", posting memes on culture well beyond hockey: https://www.hashtagsports.com/awards/shortlist-2021/devils-meme-lords
- **[V]** House of Highlights credits **consistency over any single viral hit**. The team sifts about 500 DMs a day for clips, uses relatable athlete-reaction memes, and runs Collab posts with Bleacher Report to avoid duplicate posting. Sources: https://www.ucf.edu/pegasus/house-of-highlights/ and https://www.hashtagsports.com/read/omar-raja-runs-house-of-highlights
- **[V]** The NFL says **70% of its social audience is under 35**, and it runs a creator network across TikTok, Snapchat and Instagram. Sources: https://digiday.com/media/inside-the-nfls-youth-focused-social-strategy/ and https://wsc-sports.com/blog/industry-insights/tapping-into-creator-culture-the-nfls-playbook-for-reaching-the-next-generation/

### Splash Sports (RunYourPool and OfficeFootballPool), the incumbent in office pools
- **[V]** Splash Inc. acquired RunYourPool (RYP) and OfficeFootballPool (OFP) in late 2021. At the 2023 launch of Splash Sports it cited **2.2M active players**. RYP and OFP continue as "entertainment-only" sites that charge organizers per player. Sources: https://www.sportico.com/business/sports-betting/2023/splash-inc-sports-pools-investors-1234724843/ and https://splashsports.com/blog/splash-inc-acquires-runyourpool-officefootball-pool
- **[U]** Later profiles give 80K–86K commissioners and 2–2.3M players, undated: https://yespress.io/splash-sports
- **Lesson [I]:** The commissioner is the unit of acquisition. One commissioner brings 10–100 players. Market to commissioners and let the shared board recruit the players.

### Wordle and NYT Games (share mechanics)
- **[V]** Wordle went from **90 players in Nov 2021 to more than 300K two months later**, after the emoji share grid arrived in Dec 2021. Twitter counted 3.3M people posting 32.2M tweets. Design choices were one puzzle a day, the same puzzle for everyone, no ads and no registration. The grid lets you brag without spoilers. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wordle and https://techcrunch.com/2022/05/04/wordle-new-york-times-user-growth
- **[V]** NYT Connections: more than half of new users arrived through social channels. The head of games described sharing into group chats as the driver. Source: Nieman Lab, Sep 2023, https://www.niemanlab.org/2023/09/the-new-york-times-finds-a-match-with-the-word-game-connections/
- **Lesson [I]:** Every JaxSquares board should produce a **spoiler-free, branded, screenshot-native artifact**, such as "My square: 7-0 | Q1 ✅ Q2 ❌". Group-chat sharing is the distribution channel.

### Duolingo (brand persona)
- **[V]** TikTok grew from about **50K to more than 16M** followers by Feb 2025 under social lead Zaria Parvez. Source: The Drum, Feb 25, 2025, https://www.thedrum.com/news/2025/02/25/duolingo-s-tiktok-mastermind-its-unhinged-social-strategy-and-killing-its-mascot
- **[V]** Videos without the mascot averaged about 20–30K views. Once the mascot appeared, videos "easily" hit about 1M. Source: Marketing Brew, https://www.marketingbrew.com/issues/mascottok
- **[V]** The brand leaned into user memes about the "threatening owl" instead of fighting them. Parvez, quoted: "in lore, there's always a lover, there's always enemies, there's always a struggle." Source: https://lydia.beehiiv.com/p/how-duolingo-mastered-tiktok-and-what-your-brand-can-learn. A HubSpot write-up frames the style as "unhinged", chaotic and less polished: https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/duolingo-unhinged-content
- **Lesson [I]:** A recurring character beats a logo. JaxSquares could personify the **2-2 square**: the cursed, unloved square, with running lore ("2-2 has never won in this group chat").

### Partiful (closest product analog: link-shared, no download for guests)
- **[V]** The invite is the growth loop. Recipients see the host's fun cover art and get nudged to the app. The service started on SMS and web so it worked for iPhone and Android, with native apps arriving in 2023–24. Q1 2025 averaged **500K MAU, up 400% year over year**, with 2M new users in 2025.
- **[V]** The Timothée Chalamet lookalike contest flyer carried a Partiful link and QR code. Every viral screenshot showed the link, which NoGood calls earned media "you genuinely cannot buy".
- **[V]** It was pitched in memeable shorthand as "Facebook events for hot people".
- Sources: NoGood, https://nogood.io/blog/partiful-marketing-strategy/; CNBC via NBC, https://www.nbcconnecticut.com/news/business/money-report/meet-partiful-the-gen-z-party-planning-staple-thats-taking-on-apple/3547391/; Ordinal, https://www.tryordinal.com/blog/deep-dive-how-partiful-approaches-their-social-media-strategy
- **Lesson [I]:** Make the board link preview, the board itself and the "final board" image beautiful and branded. Every group-chat drop then works as an ad, and IRL events (sports bars, watch parties) get a QR code.

### Calendly and Spotify Wrapped (in-product share loops)
- **[V]** Calendly's free-tier "Powered by Calendly" badge sits on every booking page, and recipients can create their own page right after booking. Source: https://openviewpartners.com/blog/saas-product-viral-loop/
- **[V]** Spotify builds Wrapped cards for each platform (Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat and more). It added TikTok sharing in 2021, color choices in 2020, and fill-in-the-blank effects. Sources: https://newsroom.spotify.com/tag/wrapped-share-cards and https://sproutsocial.com/insights/?p=167719
- **Lesson [I]:** Ship a 9:16 "Story card" for "I won Q2", plus a "Made with JaxSquares, run your own" footer on public boards.

**Marketing observed [V/I]:**
- Incumbents market mostly through SEO ("how to play", "best numbers", "printable template") and through commissioner tooling.

- I found **no evidence** of a squares-specific brand running a personality-led Instagram or Reels presence, which looks like white space [I].

### Positioning gaps JaxSquares can own [I]
1. **"No app, no account, no paywall."** Splash charges per grid, OfficePoolStop paywalls anything past 4 players, the iOS apps need a download (and are iPhone-only or iPad-oriented), and 10x10 requires a Facebook or email signup. DeFi Rate is the only no-signup rival, and it is **seasonal and Super Bowl-centric**.
2. **Any game, any week.** Most tools and content spike around the Super Bowl. JaxSquares can own "squares for Thanksgiving, Monday Night Football, the NBA, rivalry week and the World Series", the 51 weeks a year nobody markets to.
3. **The draw as a moment.** Splash already markets the "on camera" draw, which validates the idea. JaxSquares can make the lock and draw into a shareable reveal moment like a gender reveal (Reels-native).
4. **Winners light up automatically.** No more "wait, who has 7-4?" arguments, and the commissioner stops doing math.
5. **$0 / bragging-rights mode** is first class rather than a footnote. It unlocks office, family-with-kids, bar and creator use cases where money is legally or socially awkward (see Section 5).

## 3. Viral formats on sports Instagram in 2025–2026, and the platform mechanics behind them

### Ranking signals (design for DM sends)
- **[V]** Adam Mosseri: "One of the most important signals we use in ranking is sends per reach." He has named watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach as top signals. Sends matter most for reaching **non-followers**, which is what a new account needs. Sources: https://searchenginejournal.com/instagram-algorithm-shift-why-sends-matter-more-than-ever/521389 and https://influencermarketinghub.com/instagram-sends-per-reach-playbook/
- **[U]** The claim that sends weigh "3–5x likes" has no primary source. Don't repeat it.
- **[V]** Socialinsider Q2 2026 organic engagement rates: **carousels 0.50%**, Reels 0.48%, images 0.33%. Reels engagement has fallen two quarters running, and carousels lead most quarters. Source: https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-benchmarks/
- **[V/U]** Trial Reels show a Reel to non-followers first, with about 24h of metrics and optional auto-share within 72h. Eligibility is disputed: one source says 200+ followers for professional accounts, another says accounts under 1,000 don't have it. **Expect a brand-new account to lack it at launch.** Sources: https://www.emarketer.com/content/instagram-launches-trial-reels-empower-creators-test-new-formats and https://kapwing.com/resources/instagram-trial-reels-study-what-we-learned-after-a-trial-reel-hit-1-million-views
- **[V]** Collab posts: Schöttl's academic study of 100 collabs found reach behaves like a weighted average of the partners' reach, and **the smaller partner gains**. Buffer reports Mosseri saying collabs reach more when the **larger account initiates**. Sources: https://hstalks.com/article/11245/download/ and https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-collab-post/
- **[V]** Engagement-bait demotion (explicitly including "tag someone who…" posts) is documented for Facebook since 2017: https://lowyat.net/2017/150787/facebook-demote-engagement-bait-posts. I found no primary source for a 2025 Instagram-specific penalty [U].
  - **[I]** Prefer "send this to…" phrasing, which feeds sends, over "tag…". Keep the call to action as a caption kicker, not the whole post.

### Formats to use, with evidence level
- **Relatable memes and POV clips [V pattern].** These drive spikes for the Chargers, Devils, House of Highlights, theScore and Duolingo (sources above). Sports marketers note memes land because of their "inside-joke quality": https://www.dashsocial.com/blog/sports-marketing
- **Group-chat screenshot format [I].** No published case study found. It matches how squares actually spread (Wordle and Connections growth through group-chat sharing [V]). Use fake iMessage or group-chat mockups showing the commissioner's chaos.
- **Stat and odds carousels [V format performance + I].** Carousels lead engagement (Socialinsider). The squares odds data above is ready-made content.
- **"This or that" and polls in Stories [I].** These are low-cost, high-reply formats ("7-0 or 0-0?").
- **Reaction formats [I].** Screen-record the board as the Q4 winner lights up, with a face-cam reaction. This is similar to House of Highlights athlete reactions.
- **Micro-creator collabs [V principle].** PrizePicks found efficient creators beat big names. Collab mechanics favor the smaller partner. NFL creator content "outperformed some of our own lead content" (WSC vendor blog, undated).

## 4. Launch tactics for a bootstrapped consumer app on Instagram

1. **The product is the ad (highest leverage) [V analogs + I].** Wordle's grid, Partiful's invite cover, Calendly's badge and Spotify's share cards all show the same thing. Each shared JaxSquares board link should carry:
   - a branded OG preview image
   - a one-tap "Run your own board" call to action
   - auto-generated 4:5 and 9:16 "final board" and "I won Q3" cards with a subtle watermark
   - a QR code for TV and bar screens
2. **Commissioner-first seeding [I, informed by Splash's commissioner model].**
   - Recruit 20–30 real friend groups, fantasy league commissioners and office social committees to run a board for Weeks 8–12 and Thanksgiving.
   - Screenshot (with permission) and repost their draw moments and Q4 sweats.
   - One commissioner reaches roughly 10–100 people.
3. **Founder-led / build-in-public [V anecdotes, I].**
   - Founders tend to persuade better than brand accounts (vendor claim): https://www.socialync.io/blog/founder-build-in-public-content-playbook-2026
   - One indie founder saw Reels get about **20x** the views of identical TikToks for a global audience (anecdote): https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tips-for-building-in-public-as-an-european-founder-3ed25bde33
   - Formats: "I built this because our spreadsheet broke", "you asked, I shipped" (reply to comments with a Reel showing the feature), and weekly dev logs. Duolingo's "lore" lesson applies: give the account a recurring cast.
4. **Micro-creator partnerships [V principle from PrizePicks, I tactic].**
   - Find sports micro-creators with 5K–100K followers (team fan accounts, local sports bars, fantasy podcasters).
   - Have them run a **$0 "followers' board"** for one game. Claiming a square becomes the call to action, with a Collab post and a board link in Stories.
   - Pay a flat fee or give product. Disclose with the Paid Partnership label.
   - Per the collab research, JaxSquares, as the smaller account, gains most.
5. **Giveaways: use sparingly, with pitfalls [V/U + I].**
   - Meta Promotion Guidelines (via secondary sources) require:
     - official rules
     - eligibility (age and location)
     - a release acknowledging the promotion isn't sponsored or administered by Instagram or Meta
     - no asking people to tag themselves in content they aren't in
     - one source says no cash-for-engagement
     - Sources: https://gleam.io/blog/instagram-giveaway-rules/, https://www.viralsweep.com/blog/instagram-giveaway-rules, https://www.plexus.co/learn/trade-promotions/social-media/terms-and-conditions/instagram
   - **[U]** One vendor says a 2025 Meta update bars *requiring* likes, follows or tags as conditions of entry. This is unconfirmed, so verify against Meta's page.
   - Giveaways attract follow-and-leave and bot accounts: https://www.easypromosapp.com/blog/en/?p=65202
   - **[I]** If you run one, make the prize non-cash (a trophy, a custom "Commissioner" hoodie, a pizza for the watch party). Make the entry a free action with no purchase. Avoid "follow + tag 3" as the gate. Avoid anything resembling "win money on squares".
   - **[I, general US promo law, not verified this session]** Sweepstakes must avoid "consideration". Some states (notably NY and FL) require registration when prizes pass certain values. Keep prizes small and get a lawyer to review the rules.
6. **Bars and sports bars: a $0 angle only [V legal risk].**
   - New York's State Liquor Authority says gambling is prohibited at all liquor-licensed premises: https://www.lockportjournal.com/news/local_news/super-bowl-state-liquor-authority-warns-against-football-squares/article_077c94ba-7cba-5122-a3f5-1bbd8eb94980.html
   - Connecticut says selling squares without a license is illegal: https://www.wfsb.com/2024/01/26/buy-super-bowl-squares-state-issued-this-warning/
   - In **Wyoming**, bars are shutting down football boards after SF 44, effective Jul 1, 2026 (reported **Oct 7, 2026**): https://cowboystatedaily.com/2026/10/07/wyoming-bars-shutting-down-shake-a-days-and-football-pools-after-new-law/
   - An Illinois bar manager was sentenced for running squares (2015): https://www.shawlocal.com/2015/07/16/plainfield-bar-manager-sentenced-on-gambling-charge/a3qr5ao
   - **[I]** Pitch bars only on **free-to-play, bragging-rights boards**, such as a QR code on the TV where the winner gets a house shout-out or a free appetizer. Claiming a square must not require buying anything. Never pitch "run your bar's money pool."
7. **Office managers and HR [V].**
   - Littler (Feb 2024): squares are technically illegal in 37 states. Washington caps at one board and $1 a square. Ohio and Colorado allow pools where the organizer takes no profit. The Interstate Wire Act can come up for online, interstate pools.
   - Jackson Lewis and Littler recommend voluntary participation, nominal or **non-monetary** entry, no organizer profit, written rules, and a **free-to-play option** or non-cash prizes such as swag.
   - Sources: https://www.littler.com/news-analysis/asap/what-issues-do-super-bowl-squares-present-workplace, https://www.jacksonlewis.com/insights/super-bowl-safeguards-managing-workplace-related-risks-after-big-game, https://www.sbam.org/super-bowl-squares-and-march-madness-brackets-what-employers-need-to-know/
   - **[I]** This makes "the HR-friendly $0 office board" a clean, defensible pitch, and a LinkedIn crossover too.
8. **Reddit and Facebook groups [V/U].**
   - The common heuristic is the 9:1 (or 10:1) rule: no more than one self-promotional post in ten. Subreddit rules are stricter than the site-wide ones, and link-spamming across threads can trigger a shadowban within about 48h. Sources: https://www.indiehackers.com/post/will-i-get-banned-from-reddit-if-i-post-about-my-startup-with-1-karma-point-f023dba21a and https://somethinginc.com/blog/reddit-marketing-without-getting-banned/
   - **[I]** Contribute squares-odds data posts to fantasy and team subreddits and local Facebook groups, and answer "how do squares work" threads. Only link where a sidebar or self-promo thread allows it.
9. **Cadence [V principle from House of Highlights, Underdog and PrizePicks].** Consistency and live, in-game reactivity outperform hoping for one viral hit. **[I]** For a solo founder:
   - 4–5 feed posts a week, mixing Reels and carousels
   - daily Stories
   - live Stories during the marquee games: Sunday 1pm and 4pm windows, SNF, MNF and Thanksgiving

## 5. Compliance framing

### How competitors phrase it [V]

- RYP and OFP are "entertainment-only sites".
- Splash Squares pricing pages describe "entertainment-only contests".

### State social-gambling patterns [V; varies by state]
- **Texas** (Lubbock County DA, Feb 2025): it can fall within a defense if all winnings go to the players. There must be **no house or organizer cut**, equal chances for everyone, and a **private place** such as a home, "not a gas station or a game room". Sources: https://www.fox34.com/2025/02/05/gambling-bounds-lubbock-co-civil-da-explains-legality-super-bowl-squares/ and https://sanangelolive.com/node/78723
- **Arizona** (AG): legal if all the money goes to players and no one else benefits. Source: https://www.12news.com/article/news/local/super-bowl-square-pools-legal-in-az-mostly/75-184965136
- **Rhode Island:** a bill reintroduced in Jan 2026 would allow squares with a social relationship and nothing of value going to the host. Not enacted per the results. Source: https://www.rilegislature.gov/pressrelease/Lists/PressReleases/DispForm.aspx?ID=372290
- **Michigan:** a 2024 bill (SB 511) proposed a "bona fide social relationship" exception. It was unclear whether it passed. Source: https://igamingbusiness.com/legal-compliance/bona-fide-michigan-sports-betting-bill-introduced/
- **Littler:** illegal in 37 states absent an exception (Feb 2024).
- **[I] JaxSquares framing that fits these patterns:**
  - "JaxSquares never touches money. No fees, no cut, no house."
  - "Buy-ins are just numbers you type in, or set to $0 for bragging rights."
  - "Know your local rules."
  - The **$0 default for public or creator boards** is the cleanest posture.

### Meta / Instagram rules
- **[V] Ads.** Meta defines "online gambling and games" as products where **anything of monetary value is part of the method of entry *and* the prize**. Ads for them need **prior written permission**. Entirely free games with no chance to win anything of monetary value are excluded. All ads must target 18+ (social casino ads too). Ads that **link to pages promoting gambling** may need permission even when no gambling happens on the page. Since Jul 2025, real-money gaming ads go through "Permissions and Verifications" with manual review. Sources: https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/gambling-games, https://www.covers.com/industry/meta-moves-to-restrict-gambling-ads-on-facebook-and-instagram-july-9-2025, https://ifrahlaw.com/ifrah-on-igaming/navigating-the-high-stakes-world-of-online-gaming-advertising-policies-permissions-and-enforcement
  - **[I]** Keep ad creative and landing page squarely on **$0 / bragging rights / "we don't handle money"**. Don't show dollar pots or "payout" screens in ads. Target 18+ anyway.
- **[V] Branded content.** Brands *and* creators need written permission from Meta to promote **real-money gambling**. Source: https://indicator.media/p/this-network-of-ai-thirst-traps-repeatedly-promoted-a-gambling-website
  - **[I]** Creator collabs should show $0 boards and never feature cash pots.
- **[V/U] Recommendations.** Instagram's Recommendation Guidelines are stricter than its Community Guidelines. Content that violates them can still reach followers but "won't be recommended" to non-followers, and repeated violations can make a whole account ineligible. The excerpt names regulated products such as tobacco and vaping. **I could not confirm whether gambling is named [U].** Source: https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators
  - **[I]** Because non-follower reach is everything for a new account, be conservative with gambling-coded language and imagery (cash stacks, "winnings").
- **[U] Words.** I found **no evidence** that Instagram suppresses the word "bet" in sports content. Shadowban lists from vendor blogs are unreliable.
  - **[I] Precautionary vocabulary:**

    | Prefer | Avoid in ads and branded posts |
    |---|---|
    | squares, pool, board, buy-in (sparingly), prize split, bragging rights, "who's owed what", trophy, loser does dishes | bet, wager, gamble, odds-to-win-money, "cash out", "get paid", "free money", "guaranteed" |

  - Odds and stat carousels are fine framed as **probability or "hit rate"**, for example "how often each square hits".

### Trademarks
- **[V] "Super Bowl."** The NFL judges uses case by case. It notifies first, then sends a cease-and-desist.
  - Whole Foods used "Big Game party" instead.
  - A church was forced to cancel a "Super Bowl Bash" (2007).
  - News and editorial use has fair-use latitude, and brand promotional use does not.
  - Sources: https://www.foxnews.com/sports/businesses-steer-clear-of-using-super-bowl-moniker.amp, https://prdaily.com/can-your-brand-use-the-term-super-bowl-on-social-media, https://www.techdirt.com/articles/20200130/10363443827/that-time-year-no-nfl-cant-stop-every-business-using-super-bowl-every-instance.shtml
- **[V] Foster Garvey (2026 guidance).** Don't use "Super Bowl" or "Super Sunday" to promote events ("XYZ's Super Bowl Party").
  - **All NFL team names, nicknames and logos are registered marks**, and "even using team colors could draw the ire of the NFL".
  - Workarounds include "The Big Game", "The Big Matchup", "Football's Favorite Day", and references to the date or the cities.
  - One marketing-law blog also lists "Gameday" and "Back to Football" as protected.
  - Sources: https://www.foster.com/wp-content/uploads/pdf-exports/play-it-smart-on-game-day-a-legal-guide-to-super-bowl-marketing-12242.pdf, https://www.themotherhood.com/super-bowl-advertising-guidelines/, https://nevadabroadcasters.org/tiptoeing-on-the-sidelines-2026-update-on-super-bowl-advertising-and-promotions/
- **[V] NCAA.** "March Madness", "Final Four", "Elite Eight", "Sweet Sixteen" and "NCAA" are registered. The bracket concept is not protected. School names are probably nominative fair use, but **logos, mascots and colors are risky**. The NCAA sued DraftKings in Mar 2026, and the TRO was **denied** Mar 27, 2026. Sources: https://www.mondaq.com/unitedstates/trademark/382068/madness-in-march and https://www.wsls.com/sports/2026/03/27/federal-judge-denies-ncaas-restraining-order-request-to-make-draftkings-stop-using-march-madness/
- **[I] Practical rules for JaxSquares content:**
  - Say "the Big Game", "Thanksgiving football", "Turkey Day triple-header", "Fall Classic-season baseball". (Verify "World Series" and "Fall Classic", which are MLB marks I did not research.)
  - Name teams only factually to identify a game, for example "Bears at Lions, 1pm ET".
  - **Never** use logos, helmets, official game footage, or league fonts and colors.
  - Never imply endorsement ("Official squares of…").
  - Use **generic or illustrated** football imagery and your own screen recordings.
  - Don't use NFL broadcast clips. The leagues and networks DMCA them, and it is a trademark and copyright problem anyway.
