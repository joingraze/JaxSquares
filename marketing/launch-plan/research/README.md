# Launch research (Oct 19, 2026)

Notes from five parallel research passes that fed the launch plan. Each file
labels its claims (verified with a 2025–2026 source vs. secondary, older, or
unverified) and links its sources.

| File | What it covers |
|---|---|
| `instagram_playbook.md` | How Instagram ranks Reels/carousels/Stories now, hashtags (5 max), cadence, best times, launch tactics, ad policy |
| `sports_app_case_studies.md` | What worked for Sleeper, PrizePicks, Wordle/Connections, Partiful, Duolingo; squares competitors; compliance framing |
| `nfl_pro_calendar.md` | 2026 NFL Weeks 7–12: primetime, international and Thanksgiving games, Jaguars schedule, storylines |
| `college_calendar.md` | 2026 college slates Oct 24 – Nov 28, AP poll, Florida–Georgia move, CFP rankings dates |
| `on_this_day.md` | Verified football history for Oct 20 – Nov 29 with the square each final score paid |

Limits: the research environment could search but not open pages, so findings
come from search-result summaries of the cited pages. Anything marked
unverified, conflicting or TBA should be checked before it's posted. Kickoff
times in particular can move. The NFL schedule was cross-checked against
nflverse's `games.csv`, and every post-1999 final score used in a video was
checked against it too.
