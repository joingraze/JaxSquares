## 1. How Instagram ranking works now

### 1.1 Core signals

- **[V] Mosseri named three signals, Jan 2025.** In the first video of his 2025 series, he said the top three ranking signals are **watch time, likes and sends**.
  - He told creators to track **average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach** in Insights.
  - His exact words: *"Likes are slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content."* In his terms, connected means followers and unconnected means non-followers.
  - Sources: [Social Media Today, Jan 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/), [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-reveals-key-factors-for-boosting-reach-8646916).
- **[S] Sends per reach tracks reach most closely.** Mosseri has also said this signal correlates with overall reach more than any other, per a German report that dates the remark to spring 2024 ([onlinemarketing.de](https://onlinemarketing.de/social-media-marketing/warum-instagram-weiterhin-likes-fokussiert)).
- **[S] The "sends are worth 3–5× a like" claim has no primary source.** Many 2026 blogs repeat it (for example [posteverywhere.ai](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/how-the-instagram-algorithm-works) and [dataslayer.ai](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers)). Do not cite it as fact.
- **[S] Mosseri now points creators to engagement rate rather than raw views.** A 2026 video series, reported by [t3n](https://t3n.de/news/reels-instagram-bewerten-mosseri-1744320/), says he advised:
  - watching engagement rate (likes, comments, saves, shares, sends);
  - checking connected vs. unconnected reach;
  - using Trial Reels;
  - posting only at a pace you can sustain.

  One blog dates the series to Sept 24, 2026, but the date is not confirmed.

### 1.2 Each surface ranks differently

The baseline is Mosseri's "Instagram Ranking Explained" ([about.instagram.com, May 31, 2023](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)). **[OLD]** It is still the official framework.

| Surface | What it predicts or weighs | Reaches non-followers? |
|---|---|---|
| **Reels** | Whether you will watch to the end (watch time and completion), reshare or send, like, or go to the audio page. Also the viewer's own activity and history with the creator. | **Yes. This is the main discovery surface** (Reels tab, Explore, Suggested posts in Feed). |
| **Feed (photos and carousels)** | Past interactions with the author, relationship strength, and the chance to like, comment, save, share or tap the profile. Thousands of signals in total. | Mostly followers. Non-followers see it through "Suggested" posts. |
| **Carousels** | Same as Feed, plus swipe-through and dwell time. [PK] Instagram will re-show an unengaged carousel with the next slide first. | Some reach through Suggested posts and Explore. |
| **Stories** | Chance to tap the story, reply or skip. Based on viewing history, engagement history and closeness. | **Followers almost only.** Exposure grows through reshares and "Add Yours" chains. |
| **Explore** | Chance to like, save or share, for accounts the viewer has *not* interacted with. | Yes. Non-followers are the whole point. |

**Engagement and reach benchmarks by format:**

- **[S] Socialinsider Q2 2026 engagement rates:** carousels **0.50%** (Q1: 0.52%), Reels **0.48%** (Q1: 0.50%), images **0.33%** (Q1: 0.35%). Reels have now declined two quarters in a row ([Socialinsider 2026 benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-benchmarks/)).
- **[S] Reach rate:** Reels reach about **30.8%**, roughly double carousels (about 10–15%) and images (about 13%). This is Socialinsider data relayed by secondary blogs ([blckalpaca](https://blckalpaca.at/en/knowledge-base/social-media/social-media-algorithms-distribution/reels-vs-carousel-instagram-formats), [contentdrips, Jun 2026](https://contentdrips.com/blog/2026/06/instagram-reels-vs-carousels-2026-guide/)).
- **[S] Buffer (4M+ posts):** Reels got **36% more reach** than carousels and **125% more** than single images. Carousels led on engagement, **12% ahead** of Reels.
- **Takeaway [J]:** Use **Reels for reach** to non-followers, and **carousels for engagement, saves and explaining** things to people who already found you.

### 1.3 2025–2026 changes, by topic

**Originality and reposted content**

- **[OLD/PK] April 30, 2024 baseline.** When Instagram finds identical copies, it recommends only the original. Accounts that repost others' content without material edits get removed from recommendations. The commonly cited threshold is about 10 reposts in 30 days.
- **[V] Crackdown extended, April 30, 2026.** Instagram widened the rule: accounts that "primarily post unoriginal content" lose recommendation eligibility, now for **Reels, photos *and* carousels**.
  - Content that has been "materially edited" counts as original.
  - A watermark or a speed change does *not* count as a material edit.
  - Existing followers still see the posts. What is lost is reach to non-followers.
  - Sources: [TechCrunch, Apr 30, 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/), [Digital Music News, May 1, 2026](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2026/05/01/instagram-debuts-more-original-content-protections-for-creators/).
- **[S] Recovery and threshold details are unconfirmed.** Coverage mentions a rolling 30-day window for regaining eligibility, and a figure of about 10 unoriginal reposts in 30 days. Neither appears in Instagram's own wording as quoted.
- **[V] Mosseri's Dec 31, 2025 year-end memo.** He argued that as AI makes polish cheap, raw content signals a real person. He listed "continue to improve ranking for originality" as a priority. Strict trackers note this announced **no specific ranking change**.
- **[V] Watermarks from other apps.** Mosseri: *"we try to not recommend Reels with logos from other apps, but if it's your own logo, don't worry about it"* ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-including-your-own-logo-on-a-reel-is-ok/730852/)). Never post a Reel with a TikTok or CapCut watermark.

**Trial Reels**

- **[V] Launch.** Rolled out to all professional accounts in **December 2024**, after tests that began in May 2024. Mosseri said it was to *"depressurize the experience of sharing reels"*.
- **How a trial works:**
  - It is shown to non-followers first.
  - It is hidden from your grid, Reels tab and followers' feeds, although a follower can still see it if someone DMs it to them.
  - Results appear after about 24 hours.
  - You can share it to everyone by hand, or turn on auto-share, which triggers if it performs well in the first 72 hours.
- **[S] Scheduling.** Trial Reels reportedly became schedulable in **Feb 2026** ([WERSM](https://wersm.com/?p=117379)).
- **[S] Instagram's own claimed results:** 40% of creators who used Trial Reels started posting more often, and they saw an 80% increase in non-follower reach.
- **[S] Eligibility is unclear.** Some reports say it now requires **1,000+ followers** and a public professional account. Others say 200 followers for professional accounts. **A brand-new account may not see the "Trial" toggle**, so check the share screen.
- Sources: [netinfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/instagram-new-trial-reels/), [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-introduces-wider-access-trial-reels-9493132), [inro](https://www.inro.social/blog/what-is-a-reel-trial).

**Reel length**

- **[V] Up to 3 minutes, Jan 2025.** Instagram on Threads: *"Starting this week, we're letting creators in the United States publish reels up to 3 minutes long in the Reels editor."*
- **[S] Recommendation.** Reports say Reels up to 3 minutes are eligible to be recommended to non-followers.
- **[S] Longer than 3 minutes.** Claims that longer Reels (now reportedly up to 20 minutes) are *not* recommended remain unconfirmed.
- Sources: [onlinemarketing.de](https://onlinemarketing.de/social-media-marketing/3-minuten-lange-reels-instagram-learnings), [netinfluencer](https://netinfluencer.com/?p=32634).

**Hashtags: capped at 5**

- **[V] Confirmed.** On **Dec 18, 2025**, @creators posted: *"New hashtag guidance: Starting today, Instagram will allow up to 5 hashtags in a reel or post."*
  - The previous limit was 30.
  - The rollout was gradual. Some accounts were tested at 3 hashtags in late 2025.
  - Instagram: *"using fewer (up to 5) more targeted hashtags, rather than many generic ones, can improve both your content's performance and people's experience."*
  - It also asked people to avoid generic tags like #reels and #explore.
  - The cap applies to posts (which include carousels) and Reels. One source says a comment with more than 5 hashtags won't post.
  - Sources: [@creators on Threads](https://www.threads.com/@creators/post/DSalXGPCWM4/new-hashtag-guidance-starting-today-instagram-will-allow-up-to-hashtags-in-a), [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/), [BetaNews, Dec 19, 2025](https://betanews.com/2025/12/19/instagram-puts-a-limit-on-hashtag-usage/).
- **[S/PK] Hashtags mainly help classification.** They help Instagram categorize a post but don't meaningfully boost distribution. Mosseri has said versions of this since 2022.

**Search, keywords and Google indexing**

- **[V] Google indexing since July 10, 2025.** Google and Bing can index public posts from **professional accounts whose owners are 18+**.
  - Covered: Reels, carousels and photos. Stories and Highlights are not indexed.
  - It is on by default. The setting is "Allow public photos and videos to appear in search engine results".
  - Indexing is not real-time.
  - Sources: [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-indexing-on-google/), [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/instagram-content-becomes-searchable-on-google-starting-july-10/).
- **[S] What search reads.** Instagram search uses **keywords in captions, profile name and bio, and alt text**. Openmoves cites an April 2025 Business Insider interview with Mosseri. Kontentino, citing a 2026 Q&A it alone reports, says search also reads comments and the visual content itself ([openmoves](https://openmoves.com/blog/do-hashtags-still-matter/), [Kontentino](https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/instagram-search-seo/)).
- **Implication [J]:** Put the terms people actually search ("football squares", "Super Bowl squares", "squares pool", "office pool") in five places:
  - the profile Name field;
  - the bio;
  - the first line of the caption;
  - the on-screen text;
  - the alt text.

## 2. Reels: length, structure, safe zones, covers, captions and CTAs

### 2.1 Length

- **[S] Short wins on completion.** An unnamed analysis of 500 viral Reels (Oct 2024–Mar 2025) found **7–15 seconds** had the highest completion and shareability, with a noticeable drop-off past 30 seconds.
- **[S] Buffer** says shorter, engaging clips "still win most of the time". Its guidance: 90 seconds to 3 minutes suits deeper stories, with slightly lower reach.
- **[S] Metricool** says content matters more than length ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/library/instagram-reels-length), [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-reels-length)).
- **[J] Recommended lengths for JaxSquares:**

| Content type | Length |
|---|---|
| Memes, POV, reaction | 6–15 s |
| Product demo ("make a board in 20 seconds") | 15–30 s |
| Explainer ("how squares works and how numbers are drawn") | 30–60 s |

  Don't run longer than 60 seconds in this launch window.

### 2.2 Structure

- **[V] Watch time is a top signal, and Instagram now shows skip rate** (the share of viewers who leave within 3 seconds). The first 1–3 seconds therefore decide distribution.
- **[S] Unofficial skip-rate benchmarks:** under about 30–40% is healthy; over about 50% means the hook is failing.
- **[J] Template:**

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–1.5 s | A visual hook plus burned-in text, such as "Your group chat's squares pool, but nobody has to do math", or the board with winners lighting up. |
| 1.5–10 s | The payoff or demo. |
| End | The loop point, plus a soft CTA ("send this to whoever runs your pool"). |

  Also:
  - Always add on-screen text, since many people watch muted.
  - Use trending or original audio.
  - Shoot vertical 9:16.
  - Don't use borders, mostly-text slides, low resolution, or other apps' watermarks. These are [OLD] Instagram "Reels best practices" that are still valid.

### 2.3 Safe zones for a 1080×1920 Reel

| Area | Pixels | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Top** | Keep about **250–270 px** clear (14%): the "Reels" header and camera icon. | Meta ads spec [S] |
| **Bottom** | Organic: keep about **400–480 px** clear (handle, caption lines, audio ticker, Follow button). Ads: **about 670 px** (35%), because the CTA bar sits there. | Ads figure is the Meta spec [S]; the organic figure is a common estimate [S/PK] |
| **Right** | Keep about **120–160 px** clear on the right edge (x ≈ 920–1080) from about **y ≈ 900 to the bottom**: the like, comment, share, save and audio rail. Meta ads use 6% (about 65 px) on both sides. | [S] (sources conflict: 120 px vs. a 40% bottom-right block) |
| **Recommended "strict" box** for key text | **x 65–900, y 270–1250.** This box satisfies the ads spec, the right rail *and* the 3:4 grid crop. | [J] |
| **Grid crop** | The profile grid shows the center **1080×1440** (y ≈ 240–1680). | [S] |

Sources: [Hopper HQ 2026](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/), [socialync](https://www.socialync.io/tools/safe-zones/instagram-reels), [revid](https://www.revid.ai/instagram-reels-safe-zone).

**Stories (1080×1920):** keep about 250 px at the top (progress bar and handle) and about 250–340 px at the bottom (reply bar) free [S/PK].

### 2.4 Covers

- **[J] Design:**
  - Make a custom cover with a 3–6 word title inside the center 1080×1350 area. That fits both the 3:4 grid crop and the 4:5 in-feed crop.
  - Use one consistent template so the grid reads as a series ("SQUARES 101", "GAME DAY", "WINNERS").
  - Use the brand tokens: pitch green, gold.
- **[S]** You can adjust a post's grid preview after posting (Edit → Adjust preview).

### 2.5 Caption length

- **[S] The evidence is mixed:**
  - **[OLD] Socialinsider 2024 (9M posts):** shorter captions (under about 30 words) had higher engagement, and carousels did best with short captions.
  - **[S] PostPlanify:** caption length barely matters for Reels; feed posts with 301–800 characters had the best median engagement rate (4.2%).
  - An academic study found the peak around 600 characters.
- **[J] Caption guidance:**
  - Put the keyword-rich hook in the **first line**, because it is truncated after about 125 characters and it is also the Google snippet.
  - Reels: 1–3 lines.
  - Carousels: 1–3 lines plus the CTA.
  - Educational posts can run longer.
  - Use **3–5 specific hashtags**, never generic ones.

### 2.6 CTAs, and what counts as engagement bait

**Allowed and effective:**

- **[V] Send prompts.** Sends per reach is the strongest unconnected-reach signal, so "send this to your group chat" style prompts are the right lever. They should be **tied to the content**, for example "Send this to the friend who still runs squares on a napkin", not incentivized.
- **[S] Instagram's line on engagement bait.** Social Media Today relays Instagram's clarification on single-word CTAs (likely 2024, [OLD]): engagement bait is when **"the main aim is to inflate engagement counts to 'hack' reach"**, and creators who genuinely engage with their audience aren't baiting. A "comment BOARD and I'll DM you the link" CTA that actually delivers something is generally treated as legitimate ([SMT](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-advice-on-single-word-ctas-and-longer-reels/718151/)).
- **[J] Content-tied questions work.** Examples: "Which number combo do you want: 7-0 or 4-7?" or "Worst squares number ever? Wrong answers only." These invite answers that depend on the post.

**Penalized or risky:**

- **[OLD] Facebook's 2017 engagement-bait policy.** It defines vote baiting, react baiting, **share baiting ("share with 5 friends to win")**, **tag baiting ("tag a friend who…")** and **comment baiting (asking for specific words, numbers or emojis)**. Offenders get reduced reach ([TechCrunch, Dec 2017](https://techcrunch.com/2017/12/18/facebook-is-clamping-down-on-posts-that-shamelessly-beg-for-your-engagement)).
- **[PK] Instagram's Recommendation Guidelines.** They list clickbait or engagement bait, and *content promoting a contest or giveaway*, as content that may not be recommended to non-followers.
- **[J] Rules for JaxSquares:**
  - Use at most **one** CTA per post.
  - Don't stack "like + comment + tag + share".
  - Avoid making "tag 3 friends" the main point of a post.
  - A light, content-native "tag your commissioner" is low risk. Making it the whole post is not.

## 3. Cadence, timing and growth expectations

### 3.1 Posting cadence

- **[V/S] Buffer 2026 (about 2M posts; feed posts, Reels and carousels; Stories excluded):**
  - **3–5 posts a week is the sweet spot.**
  - Going from 1–2 to 3–5 posts a week roughly **doubles the follower-growth rate** and adds about 12% reach per post.
  - 6–9 posts a week gives about **3.7×**, and 10+ about 5.5×, with diminishing returns.
  - Source: [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/).
- **[S] Mosseri:** "For some people posting five times a day works great, for others once a month." Don't commit to a pace you can't sustain.
- **[S] Stories:**
  - Socialinsider 2025 benchmarks: about 3 a week for accounts with 1k–5k followers.
  - Fanpage Karma: about 1 a day for accounts with 1k–10k followers.
  - [OLD] Buffer: 1–7 a day, with completion dropping below 70% after 7.
  - Dash Social 2025: average completion is about 70%.
- **[J] JaxSquares cadence:**
  - **4–5 Reels a week**: 3 planned plus 1–2 reactive game-day posts.
  - **1–2 carousels a week**: "how it works", "squares odds by number", "rules explainer".
  - **Stories on 4–7 days a week, 2–5 frames a day.** Spike on game days with a poll or quiz before kickoff, a link sticker and a winner recap.

### 3.2 Best days and times

Treat all of these as directional and in the account's local time; studies differ on time zones. Set the US-facing schedule in **Eastern Time**.

| Study | Date and data | Best windows |
|---|---|---|
| **Sprout Social** [V/S] | Updated **Mar 2026**; about 2B engagements across 307K profiles | Mon 2–4 pm; **Tue 1–7 pm**; **Wed 12–9 pm**; Thu 12–2 pm. **Tue and Wed strongest.** Weekends weakest (Saturday lowest); no Friday peak. |
| **Buffer** [S] | 9.6M posts, 2024–25 data | Thu 9 am, Wed 12 pm, Wed 6 pm. Wednesday is the best day. |
| **Metricool** [S] | 2026, 24M+ posts | **Evenings 6–9 pm** most days. |
| **Sociality.io** [S] | Aug 2025–Jul 2026, 335K brand posts | 8 pm–midnight; **Sunday 10 pm–midnight is the single strongest window**. The hour matters more than the day. |
| Later [OLD] | 2024 data | 5 am. An outlier; likely stale. |

Sources: [Sprout](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-instagram/), [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/when-is-the-best-time-to-post-on-instagram), [Metricool](https://metricool.com/best-time-to-post-instagram/), [Sociality.io](https://sociality.io/blog/best-time-to-post-on-instagram/).

**[J] Sports overlay (Eastern Time).** Verify schedules each week.

- **NFL Sunday:** post a "lock your board" Reel or Story between **11 am and 12:30 pm**, before the 1 pm kickoffs. Post a recap or meme from the **late afternoon (4–7 pm) into the evening**. The evening matches Sociality's Sunday-night finding.
- **Thursday and Monday night games (about 8:15 pm):** post a pre-game Story at 6–7:30 pm and a reactive Reel within about 30–60 minutes of a big swing or the final whistle.
- **College football Saturday:** windows are noon, 3:30 pm and 7:30 pm.
- **Planned evergreen Reels:** use the Tue and Wed 12–7 pm windows.
- **Thanksgiving, Thu Nov 26, 2026:** three NFL games, traditionally about 12:30, 4:30 and 8:20 pm ET.
  - Post the "Thanksgiving squares" push **Mon–Wed Nov 23–25**. People set up pools before the holiday.
  - Then run Stories all day Thursday.

### 3.3 Realistic growth

- **[S] There is no solid 2025–26 dataset for brand-new accounts.**
  - A common planning benchmark is **5–10% monthly growth early**. That benchmark is not data-backed, and it barely moves an account starting from zero.
  - One anecdote: 0 to 1,000 followers in about 11 weeks.
- **[J] Expectations for Oct 26–Nov 26:**
  - **Organic only, consistent:** about **100–500 followers** after 4 weeks.
  - **One Reel breaking into non-follower distribution:** views can jump 10–100×, and the account could reach **1k+**.
  - Followers matter less here than outcomes. Track **sends per reach, unconnected reach, profile visits → link taps → boards created**.

## 4. Launch tactics for a brand-new account

**Seeding the grid**

- **[J] Before Oct 26:**
  - Switch to a **professional (Creator or Business) account**. That unlocks insights, Trial Reels, scheduling, boosts and Google indexing for 18+ owners.
  - Pre-post **6–9 items** so the 3:4 grid looks alive from the first profile visit. Suggested set:
    - a pinned "What is JaxSquares" Reel;
    - a pinned "How to run a squares pool in 60 seconds" carousel;
    - a pinned "Winners light up automatically" demo;
    - 3–6 evergreen memes or explainers.
  - Pin 3 posts.
  - Keep every post **original**; reposting will get recommendations cut.

**Collab posts**

- **[S] How they work:**

  - Works for Feed posts, carousels and Reels; not Stories.
  - Collaborators must accept the invite.
  - Sources: [Vista Social](https://vistasocial.com/insights/instagram-collab-posts/), [measure.studio](https://www.measure.studio/blog/how-to-use-instagram-collab-posts).

  - local sports bars;

**Micro-creator partnerships**

**Giveaways**

- **[S/OLD] Instagram's Promotion Guidelines are long-standing, pre-2025.** A giveaway must have:

  The host is responsible for legal compliance. Sources: [Woobox 2026](https://blog.woobox.com/instagram-giveaway-rules-to-remember-checklist-if-you-forget/), [Gleam](https://gleam.io/blog/instagram-giveaway-rules/).

- **[PK] Giveaway posts are generally not recommended to non-followers.** They mainly juice engagement from people who already see you.
- **[J] For JaxSquares:**

## 5. Sports-moment content

**Real-time posting**

- **[S]** Dash Social and ShortStack report that sports audiences follow and comment live. Reels and Stories fit the pace. Memes perform strongly with superfans. Sports brands average about 20 IG posts a week ([Dash Social](https://www.dashsocial.com/blog/sports-marketing)).
- **[S]** There is no rigorous public study on sports share or DM rates.
- **[J] Game-day loop:**
  - Pre-game Story: poll plus link sticker.
  - Each quarter: a Story, "Q1 winner: whoever had 7-3".
  - Post-game: Reel or meme within about 30–60 minutes.
  - Next morning: carousel recap ("The most cursed squares numbers this week").

**Share-driving formats [J]**

- "POV: you got 2-5 in the squares pool"
- "When the kicker misses the PAT and ruins your square"
- "Types of people in every squares pool" (carousel)
- "Send this to your commissioner"
- "Rank the numbers" tier lists
- Light "tag the friend who…" lines inside the content

All of these are original, people-relatable and send-oriented. For the visuals, use the app's own UI and real reactions from your team.

**Names, logos and footage: trademark and copyright** (not legal advice)

- **[S/OLD] "Super Bowl."** The NFL holds multiple "SUPER BOWL" registrations and polices **commercial** use.
  - Nominative fair use is narrow in advertising.
  - Small brands typically say "**the Big Game**".
  - Don't imply NFL endorsement or sponsorship.
  - Sources: [PR Daily](https://www.prdaily.com/can-your-brand-use-the-term-super-bowl-on-social-media/), [FindLaw](https://www.findlaw.com/?p=43461), [Small Biz Trends](https://smallbiztrends.com/super-bowl-trademark-rules/). These are mostly 2014–2019 articles with no 2025 changes found.
- **[S] NCAA marks.** Registered marks include March Madness, Final Four, Elite Eight, The Big Dance, Selection Sunday, and NCAA Sweet 16. Unauthorized commercial or promotional use invites cease-and-desist letters; contests that use NCAA marks draw scrutiny ([Nevada Broadcasters, 2026 update](https://nevadabroadcasters.org/its-march-time-for-madness-risks-of-using-or-accepting-or-engaging-in-advertising-or-promotions-that-use-final-four-or-other-ncaa-trademarks-2026-update-par/)).
- **[OLD] Game footage and highlights.** The NFL has issued DMCA takedowns of highlight clips and GIFs (2015 Twitter episode). [PK] Meta Rights Manager auto-matches broadcast content.
- **[J] Risk ladder:**

| Risk | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Generally OK** | Team names and matchups in plain text or captions to identify a game ("Chiefs vs. Bills squares board"), dates and scores, a generic "football" look. Add "Not affiliated with the NFL, NCAA or any team" in the bio or footer. |
| **Use care** | "Super Bowl" in organic captions or hashtags (low but nonzero risk; avoid in ads and in the brand name). NCAA tournament terms (avoid). Player names in promotional context (right of publicity). |
| **Not OK** | Team or league **logos**, helmets, uniforms as trade dress, **broadcast footage or audio**, official highlight reposts, filming a TV broadcast, NFL Films clips, player photos. All of these risk takedowns or strikes, and they break the originality rules anyway. |

## 6. Paid boosts

**Is a small budget worth it?**

- **[S]** Boosting extends reach but converts weakly into followers ([Hootsuite $100 experiment](https://blog.hootsuite.com/experiment-instagram-post-promotion/)).
- **[S]** Boost only posts that are already performing above average organically. About $5 a day is the minimum for usable learning ([SocialBu](https://socialbu.com/blog/boost-post-on-instagram)).
- **[J] Plan:**
  - Weeks 1–2 organic only.
  - Week 3 (Nov 9–15): **$50–150** behind the 1–2 Reels with the best **sends per reach** and watch time. Optimize for profile visits or link clicks, not followers.
  - Week 4: a Thanksgiving pre-push, **Nov 22–25**.
  - Judge on **cost per board created** (UTM link).

**Gambling policy**

- **[V] Meta's "Online gambling and games" ad policy.** Ads for any product where **"anything of monetary value is included as part of a method of entry and prize"** need **prior written permission**.
  - That list explicitly includes **fantasy sports, sweepstakes, skill-game tournaments**, betting, lotteries and raffles.
  - Approval is tied to specific URLs and territories.
  - Ads are 18+ only.
  - Landing pages that promote gambling are covered too.
  - Free-to-play games are reportedly exempt.
  - Source: [Meta Transparency Center policy page](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/gambling-games), [Ifrah Law](https://ifrahlaw.com/ifrah-on-igaming/navigating-the-high-stakes-world-of-online-gaming-advertising-policies-permissions-and-enforcement).
- **[J] What this means for JaxSquares.** The app is free and doesn't handle money, so it plausibly sits on the free-to-play side. **But** a boost whose creative or landing page shows buy-ins, payouts or "$10 a square" is likely to be **rejected or flagged as gambling**. Boosted posts go through ad review against Advertising Standards; organic posts don't. For any **paid** content:
  - Frame it as a "free game-day party game, play for bragging rights".
  - No dollar amounts and no "bet", "wager" or "payout".
  - Target 18+.
  - Keep the landing page free of money language.
- **[PK/J] Organic vs. paid.** Organic posts follow Community Guidelines and Recommendation Guidelines, not the ad policies, so normal talk about office pools is fine organically. Still, avoid soliciting money from strangers in posts or DMs, and keep payouts off the brand's content.
