## Research status (read first)

- **Verified:** 27 moments on 21 of the 37 calendar dates (Oct 24 – Nov 29) have their date and final score checked against at least one reliable source, and nearly all also have the key goal minute. Section A's table contains only these. Section B has 12 sourced fun facts.
- **How it was checked:** about 37 web searches. Direct page fetches failed again: DNS errors and a proxy 403 (Wikipedia, mlssoccer.com, ESPN's API). Every "verified" item rests on search-result summaries of the cited pages, so **open the link before posting a number in a caption**, minutes especially.
- **Not checked:** everything under "Unverified queue" was written from memory. Don't post it until a source confirms it.
- **Confidence:** High means two reliable sources agree on the date, the score and the minute that sets the square. Medium means one source only, or sources disagree on a detail; the row says which.
- **Square rules used (the app's own, from `shared/soccer.ts`):**
  - A square is the goal's minute as the match clock shows it. The clock counts the minute in progress, so a goal 8 seconds in reads **1'** (square **01**) and one at 72 seconds reads **2'** (square **02**).
  - First-half stoppage time counts as the 45th minute: 45+2' is square **45**.
  - Second-half stoppage time runs on: 90+3' is square **93**.
  - Extra time keeps its last two digits: 107' is square **07**, and 120+8' (128') is square **28**. First-half stoppage time in extra time counts as the 105th minute (square 05).
  - No goals: square **00** wins.
  - Penalty-shootout kicks aren't goals.
  - A board can award the first goal's minute, every goal's minute and the last goal's minute, so each row names the first and last goal where they're known.
- **Wording:** follows the product rules in CLAUDE.md. Captions should say "square", "wins" and "prize".

**Square patterns worth a carousel:**
- **Square 28, twice in one match.** In MLS Cup 2022 the first goal (Acosta 28') and the last goal (Bale 120+8' = 128') both won square 28.
- **Square 00 belongs to big nights too.** Italy 0, Sweden 0 (Nov 13, 2017) and USA 0, England 0 (Nov 25, 2022).
- **The 01 square, from fast starts.** Hidegkuti 1' (1953), Gualtieri at 8.3 seconds (1993), and the fastest Premier League and World Cup goals (Section B).
- **The 45 square takes all first-half stoppage time.** Ben Olsen (MLS Cup 1999) and Schweinsteiger (Nov 28, 2015) both won 45 without scoring in the 45th minute.
- **The 90s row is stoppage-time drama.** Alex Morgan 94, Altidore 93, Bellingham 92.
