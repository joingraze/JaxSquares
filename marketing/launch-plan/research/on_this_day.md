## 2. SQUARES FUN FACTS

### Verified
Each fact below is either arithmetic on the verified scores above or comes from the cited source.

- **The cursed 9/7 square.** Three of the wildest finishes in NFL history ended **19-17**: Dempsey's 63-yarder (Nov 8, 1970), the Miracle at the Meadowlands (Nov 19, 1978) and Tucker's 66-yarder against Detroit (Sept 26, 2021). The Lions lost 19-17 on two different record-breaking kicks. Sources are as cited in the table, plus https://au.sports.yahoo.com/cursed-the-lions-have-lost-twice-19-17-on-an-nfl-record-kick-194314895.html. **High.**
- **The Lions' Thanksgiving 9/6 bookends.** Detroit's first Thanksgiving game (Bears 19-16, 1934) and the 1998 coin-toss game (Lions 19-16 OT) both ended **19-16**. **High.**
- **0/0 legends.** Red Grange's NFL debut (0-0, Thanksgiving 1925) and the 1966 Notre Dame vs. Michigan State "Game of the Century" (10-10) both paid the 0/0 square. **High.**
- **The 2/1 square has the biggest offensive days.** The highest-scoring NFL game ever (Redskins 72-41, 113 points), Dub Jones' 6-TD game (42-21) and Jason Garrett's Thanksgiving comeback (42-31) all land on 2/1. **High.**
- **The 6/4 square goes back to 1869.** The first college game (Rutgers 6-4), the Cowboys' first Thanksgiving game (26-14) and the Leon Lett game (16-14) all paid 6/4. **High.**
- **Mirror-image square.** In the Monday Night Miracle the 3rd quarter ended MIA 30, NYJ 7 and the game ended NYJ 40, MIA 37. Both are 0 and 7, but on opposite teams' axes, so they are two different squares (Oct 23, 2000). **High.**
- **Heidi Game swing.** When NBC cut away, the Jets led 32-29 (a 2/9 square). The final was 43-32 (3/2), and the square changed twice in the last 42 seconds. **High.**
- **Record scorers with tiny digits.** Payton's record 275-yard game ended 10-7 (0/7). Ernie Nevers' 40-point game ended 40-6 (0/6). **High.**
- **The Jaguars' record kick paid 0/9** (30-29 in OT, Nov 2, 2025). **High.**
- **First 50/50 game.** Rams 54, Chiefs 51 (Nov 19, 2018) was the first NFL game in which both teams scored 50 or more. It paid the 4/1 square. Source: https://abc7.com/sports/rams-outgun-chiefs-in-wild-mnf-shootout/4722878/. **High.**
- **Scorigami count.** **1,093 unique final scores in NFL history** as of late September 2025: https://www.click2houston.com/news/local/2025/09/29/nfl-scorigami-the-fans-that-cheer-for-chaos-above-all. **Medium**, because it is a single source and the number changes almost weekly. ## 3. THANKSGIVING FOOTBALL TRIVIA

### Verified
- **Why Detroit:** George A. Richards bought the Portsmouth Spartans in 1934, moved them to Detroit and renamed them the Lions. Attendance had been weak, so he staged a Thanksgiving game against the unbeaten Bears and got the 94-station NBC radio network to air it nationally. The Bears won 19-16 on **Nov 29, 1934**. Sources: https://profootballhof.com/football-history/thanksgiving-and-the-nfl and https://detroithistorical.org/blog/2015-11-25-first-thanksgiving-day-football-game-detroit. **High.**
- **Why Dallas:** Tex Schramm volunteered the Cowboys in 1966, and the league agreed to guarantee the gate. Dallas beat Cleveland 26-14 on **Nov 24, 1966** in front of a team-record crowd of about 80,000. The Cowboys have hosted every year since, except 1975 and 1977, when the St. Louis Cardinals hosted. Sources: https://sportsday.dallasnews.com/dallas-cowboys/cowboys/2017/11/23/real-reason-cowboys-lions-play-home-every-thanksgiving-day and https://www.cnbc.com/2018/11/21/why-the-dallas-cowboys-play-football-on-thanksgiving.html. **High.**
- **The first NFL superstar debuted on Thanksgiving:** Red Grange, Bears 0, Cardinals 0, 1925. Source: https://profootballhof.com/news/grange-s-debut-on-thanksgiving. **High.**
- **Most points by one player in an NFL game** were scored on Thanksgiving: Ernie Nevers' 40, on Nov 28, 1929. **High.**
- **The most famous Thanksgiving blunders:** Leon Lett, 1993 (Dolphins 16-14); the coin toss, 1998 (Lions 19-16 OT, which changed the rules); the Butt Fumble, 2012 (Patriots 49-19). **High.**
- **The fastest Thanksgiving finish:** Dave Williams' 95-yard OT kickoff return (Bears 23-17, 1980). **High.**
- **A college Thanksgiving classic:** Nebraska 35, Oklahoma 31, "Game of the Century," Nov 25, 1971. **High.**
- **The Thanksgiving rushing record:** O.J. Simpson's 273 yards (1976, in a 27-14 loss to Detroit). **High.**
