## Most squares-worthy games in the window

1. **Thu Nov 26, Chiefs at Bills (8:20, NBC).** Thanksgiving primetime between two MVP-level QBs, with unbeaten KC and the first Thanksgiving game in Buffalo.
2. **Thu Nov 26, Eagles at Cowboys (4:30, FOX).** Thanksgiving's biggest-audience slot and a rivalry, with Hurts' blood clot as the subplot.
3. **Thu Nov 26, Bears at Lions (1:00, CBS).** Opens the Thanksgiving tripleheader; one board per game makes it easy to sell a "turkey triple."
4. **Wed Nov 25, Packers at Rams (8:00, Netflix).** First-ever Thanksgiving Eve game and the natural Friendsgiving squares night.
5. **Thu Nov 5, Jaguars at Ravens (8:15, Prime).** The home team's only primetime game in the window, and the core Jacksonville content moment.
6. **Sun Nov 22, Vikings vs. 49ers in Mexico City (SNF 8:20, NBC).** Possibly a clash of unbeatens (status as of Oct 19), in primetime from abroad.
7. **Fri Nov 27, Broncos at Steelers (3:00, Prime).** Black Friday afternoon game with Rodgers' final-season storyline.
8. **Mon Oct 26, Cowboys at Eagles (MNF, ESPN/ABC).** First big rivalry game in the window, with the Eagles in crisis.
9. **Sun Oct 25, Chiefs at Seahawks (SNF, NBC).** Unbeaten KC in primetime; Paris (Rodgers vs. Manning record) plays that morning.
10. **Sun Nov 29, Titans at Jaguars (4:05, CBS).** Home Jaguars game on Thanksgiving weekend, as the "leftovers" board.
