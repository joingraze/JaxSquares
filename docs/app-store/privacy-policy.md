**JaxSquares Privacy Policy**
Last updated: <date>

JaxSquares lets friends play football squares together. We built it to need as
little of your information as possible. There are no accounts, no ads and no
tracking.

## What we collect

- **A random device ID.** The app creates a random identifier the first time
  you open it and stores it on your device. We use it to recognize your squares
  and the boards you host or join. It isn't your Apple or advertising
  identifier.
- **The name you choose.** The display name, color and emoji you pick for a
  board. Everyone in that board can see them. A nickname is fine.
- **Board content.** Board names, team names, the optional per-quarter notes a
  host writes, and the scores and results of the game.
- **Notification tokens.** If you turn on notifications, we store the push
  token your device or browser gives us. We use it to tell you about the boards
  you're in, and you can turn notifications off at any time.
- **Purchase records.** When a host pays to create a board, we keep the
  transaction reference, amount and board it unlocked. Card and Apple Pay
  details go straight to Apple or Stripe. We never see them.
- **Basic server logs.** When your device talks to our servers, our hosting
  provider records technical details like IP address and request time. We use
  them to keep the service running and secure. We don't use them to work out
  where you are.

We don't collect your email, phone number, contacts, photos, location or
health data. We don't use analytics or advertising SDKs.

## How we use it

We use it only to run JaxSquares:

- showing boards
- drawing numbers
- recording scores and winners
- sending the notifications you asked for
- unlocking boards that were paid for
- preventing abuse

We don't sell your information, share it for advertising, or track you across
other apps or websites.

## Who processes it for us

| Provider | What it does |
|---|---|
| Cloudflare | Hosting and database |
| Apple | In-App Purchase and push notification delivery |
| Google Firebase Cloud Messaging | Delivering push notifications to the app |
| Stripe | Web payments for board creation |

Each provider handles data under its own privacy terms, and only for these
purposes.

## Sharing inside a board

Anyone with a board's link can see:

- that board's name and notes
- the players' display names, colors and emoji
- which squares each player claimed
- the scores and winners

Only share links with people you want to play with.

## How long we keep it

- **Boards and their data.** We keep these until the host deletes the board.
- **Purchase records.** We keep these as long as tax and accounting law
  requires.
- **Push tokens.** We delete a token when you turn notifications off, or when
  Apple or Google tells us it's no longer valid.

## Your choices

- **Leave a board.** Release your squares before the numbers are drawn.
- **Delete a board.** The host can delete a board at any time.
- **Notifications.** Turn them off in the app, or in iOS Settings → JaxSquares.
- **Start fresh.** Delete the app, or clear the site's data in your browser,
  and the device ID is removed from your device.
- **Ask us.** To ask what we hold or to have it deleted, email
  support@jaxsquares.com with a board link. We'll respond within 30 days.

## Children

JaxSquares isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect
information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. If a change matters, we'll
also tell you in the app.
