**JaxSquares Privacy Policy**
Last updated: <date>

JaxSquares lets friends play football squares together. We built it to need as
little of your information as possible. You don't need an account, and there
are no ads and no tracking.

## What we collect

- **A random device ID.** The app creates a random identifier the first time
  you open it and stores it on your device. We use it to recognize your squares
  and the boards you host or join. It isn't your Apple or advertising
  identifier.
- **If you sign in (optional).** Signing in with Apple or Google gives us the
  account ID they assign you and, if you share it, your email. We use them only
  to keep your boards on every device you sign in on, and we show your email
  only to you. Sign in with Apple lets you hide your email.
- **The name you choose.** The display name, color and emoji you pick for a
  board. Everyone in that board can see them. A nickname is fine.
- **Board content.** Board names, team names, the optional per-quarter notes a
  host writes, and the scores and results of the game.
- **Notification tokens.** If you turn on notifications, we store the push
  token your device or browser gives us. We use it to tell you about the boards
  you're in, and you can turn notifications off at any time.
- **Email for a board (optional).** If you ask for email about a board, we
  keep the address you type. We send one email to confirm it, then only when
  the numbers are drawn, if you win, and the final results. Only you see it.
- **Purchase records.** When a host pays the host fee for a board (when they
  draw its numbers), we keep the transaction reference, amount and the board it
  paid for. Card and Apple Pay
  details go straight to Apple or Stripe. We never see them.
- **An Annual Pass (optional).** If you get the yearly Annual Pass, we keep its
  subscription reference (from the App Store or Stripe), whether it's active,
  when it renews or ends, and whether it renews, tied to your device ID or
  account. For a pass bought on the web, Stripe also keeps a customer record
  for it. We use this only to cover your boards' host fee and to show your
  pass.
- **An Annual Pass + Venue and its club (optional).** If you get the yearly Annual Pass + Venue, we
  keep it like an Annual Pass, plus your club: the name and logo you give it (the
  logo is resized on your device before it's uploaded) and a scrambled
  fingerprint of its TV link, never the link itself. The club's name and logo
  are public on the boards you host, their TV view, printout and link
  previews. They're deleted with your account.
- **Basic server logs.** When your device talks to our servers, our hosting
  provider records technical details like IP address and request time. We use
  them to keep the service running and secure. We don't use them to work out
  where you are.

We don't collect your phone number, contacts, photos (other than a club logo
an Annual Pass + Venue holder chooses to upload), location or health data, or your email
unless you sign in and share it or ask for email about a board. We don't use analytics or advertising SDKs.

## How we use it

We use it only to run JaxSquares:

- showing boards
- drawing numbers
- recording scores and winners
- sending the notifications and emails you asked for
- letting hosts draw the numbers once a board is paid for, or covered by their
  Annual Pass or Annual Pass + Venue
- showing an Annual Pass + Venue holder's club on their boards, and a players list (names,
  squares and when each joined) to the board's host and co-hosts
- keeping your boards on every device you sign in on
- preventing abuse

We don't sell your information, share it for advertising, or track you across
other apps or websites.

## Who processes it for us

| Provider | What it does |
|---|---|
| Cloudflare | Hosting and database |
| Apple | In-App Purchase and subscriptions, push notification delivery and Sign in with Apple |
| Google | Sign in with Google (optional) |
| Google Firebase Cloud Messaging | Delivering push notifications to the app |
| Stripe | Web payments for the host fee, the Annual Pass and the Annual Pass + Venue |
| Resend | Sending the board emails you ask for |

Each provider handles data under its own privacy terms, and only for these
purposes.

## Sharing inside a board

Anyone with a board's link can see:

- that board's name and notes
- the players' display names, colors and emoji
- which squares each player claimed
- the scores and winners

Only share links with people you want to play with.

## How long we keep it

- **Boards and their data.** We keep these until the host deletes the board.
- **Your account.** We keep it until you delete it. Your boards and squares
  stay on their boards afterwards, no longer tied to you.
- **A club's name and logo.** We keep them until you change them or delete
  your account. They stop showing on your boards when the Annual Pass + Venue ends.
- **Purchase records.** We keep these as long as tax and accounting law
  requires.
- **Push tokens.** We delete a token when you turn notifications off, or when
  Apple or Google tells us it's no longer valid.
- **Email for a board.** We delete the address a week after the board's game
  ends, as soon as you unsubscribe or turn it off, or after 7 days if you
  never confirm it. If you unsubscribe from all JaxSquares email, we keep only
  a scrambled fingerprint of the address so we never email it again.

## Your choices

- **Leave a board.** Release your squares before the numbers are drawn.
- **Delete a board.** The host can delete a board at any time.
- **Notifications.** Turn them off in the app, or in iOS Settings → JaxSquares.
- **Email.** Every email has an unsubscribe link, for that board or for all
  JaxSquares email. You can also turn it off from the board's bell.
- **Sign out or delete your account.** In Profile → Account. Signing out takes
  your boards off that device. Deleting your account removes your sign-in,
  email and device links, and revokes JaxSquares' access to your Apple ID.
  Your boards and squares stay on their boards for the other players.
- **Start fresh.** Delete the app, or clear the site's data in your browser,
  and the device ID is removed from your device.
- **Ask us.** To ask what we hold or to have it deleted, email
  support@jaxsquares.com with a board link. We'll respond within 30 days.

## Children

JaxSquares isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect
information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. If a change matters, we'll
